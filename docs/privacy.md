**Effective date: October 18, 2026**

PerqPal is developed by Andrew Fomichev. This policy explains how the PerqPal iOS and iPadOS app handles information.

## Information stored on your device

PerqPal lets you select credit cards, add custom names, record benefit usage and claimed amounts, write notes, configure reminders, and change app settings. This content is stored locally on your device using Apple platform storage. PerqPal does not operate an account system and does not upload this card and benefit content to a PerqPal server.

Local data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, or remove the app and its data from your device. Removing the app is the primary way to remove all locally stored PerqPal data.

## Usage analytics

When Share Usage Analytics is enabled, PerqPal sends limited analytics to TelemetryDeck. TelemetryDeck receives a pseudonymous identifier associated with the app installation, product interactions, and general device and app information such as device type, operating-system version, app version, build number, language, region, display characteristics, and accessibility preferences.

PerqPal may send generalized values such as session-duration ranges, card and benefit count ranges, selected feature actions, notification-permission status, catalog-update status, reminder-schedule changes, and broad value or expiration ranges. Search analytics use search length and result counts rather than the search text itself.

PerqPal is designed not to send card nicknames, notes, search text, email addresses, card instance identifiers, benefit instance identifiers, card opened dates, payment-card numbers, or bank credentials to TelemetryDeck.

Analytics are used to understand feature usage, maintain reliability, and improve the app. Analytics are not used for third-party advertising or to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. You can stop future analytics at any time in PerqPal under Settings > Privacy > Share Usage Analytics.

TelemetryDeck processes analytics on PerqPal's behalf. Its privacy information is available at [telemetrydeck.com/privacy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/).

## Notifications and content updates

If you enable reminder alerts, PerqPal uses Apple local notifications to remind you about benefit deadlines. Reminder scheduling occurs on your device. PerqPal does not use remote push notifications for these reminders.

PerqPal periodically checks public files hosted on GitHub for updated card-benefit catalog data and analytics configuration. These requests do not include your locally stored card or benefit content.

## Purchases

Lifetime Pro purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. PerqPal receives transaction and entitlement status from Apple so it can unlock purchased features. PerqPal does not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Sharing and sale of information

PerqPal shares the limited analytics described above with TelemetryDeck when analytics are enabled and relies on Apple services for app distribution, purchases, notifications, and device-level privacy controls. PerqPal does not sell personal information or share locally stored card and benefit content with advertisers or data brokers.

## Retention

Local content remains on your device as described above. Analytics are retained according to TelemetryDeck's retention practices and PerqPal's TelemetryDeck configuration. Because analytics use pseudonymous identifiers, PerqPal may not be able to associate an analytics record with a specific person in response to an access or deletion request.

## Children

PerqPal is not directed to children and is intended for people managing their own credit-card benefits. PerqPal does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## International privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of personal data. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a privacy request, use the contact information below. PerqPal may need enough information to understand and respond to the request.

## Independent service and informational content

PerqPal is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any bank, card issuer, payment network, or rewards program named in the app. Card benefits, fees, eligibility, credits, deadlines, and other terms can change. Verify current details directly with the applicable issuer. PerqPal does not provide financial, legal, or tax advice.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PerqPal's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version. Material changes will be reflected in the app or on this page, as appropriate.
