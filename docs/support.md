## Common questions

### Where is my information stored?

Your selected cards, custom names, benefit usage, claimed amounts, notes, reminder preferences, and app settings are stored locally on your device. PerqPal does not require an account or connect to your bank.

### How do I restore Lifetime Pro?

Open PerqPal, go to Settings, and select Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account that made the original purchase.

### Why am I not receiving reminders?

Open PerqPal's Reminder alerts settings and confirm reminders are enabled. Then open the iOS Settings app, select Notifications > PerqPal, and confirm notifications are allowed.

### How do I stop usage analytics?

Open PerqPal and go to Settings > Privacy, then turn off Share Usage Analytics.

### How do I remove my data?

You can edit or delete cards and benefit records in the app. To remove all locally stored PerqPal data, delete the app and its data from your device.
